## How they were made and checked (reproducible)

1. `tools/voice/gen_voice.py` — style `F3` (chosen in the first round from 10 styles) × 14 lines × 6 seeded takes (84 clips).
2. `tools/voice/eval_voice.py` — every take transcribed back with faster-whisper (`small`, CPU) in its
   language; 80/84 takes were heard as the intended word (every line had at least 4 of 6). Level,
   silence and pitch movement (autocorrelation F0) were measured. (Korean 예스 is transcribed as "Yes".)
3. Style `F3` (first round: 10 styles × the earlier word lines, all Korean takes recognized).
4. `tools/voice/pick_voice.py` — per line, the take heard exactly as the line (nothing extra) with the
   most pitch movement; 90 Hz high-pass, silence trimmed, loudness normalized (about −17 dBFS RMS,
   peak ≤ −1.5 dBFS). The 14 processed files were transcribed again: 14/14 heard as intended.

`manifest.json` lists the source take, transcript and measurements of each file.

These checks are objective only. **Nobody has listened to the clips during development**; a human
listening review is still needed before release (tone, naturalness, fit with the music).
